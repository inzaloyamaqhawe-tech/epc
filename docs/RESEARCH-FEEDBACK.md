# Review of research extension feedback

Reviewed against the implementation and primary sources on 9 November 2026.

Implemented now: annual nominal NPV, IRR, escalating-tariff and discounted payback; configurable project horizon, discount rate, tariff escalation slider, annual maintenance per initiative and optional internal carbon price. Initial cost is at year zero, savings at year-end, year-one tariff is the recorded tariff, escalation starts in year two. Maintenance is constant nominal annual cost. No financing, taxes, salvage value or degradation is assumed. Carbon scenario values are excluded from NPV/IRR and cash savings. Rates are user assumptions, not Eskom forecasts. Scenario preferences persist in the same browser; they are not approved per-project financial records. Formula and edge-case tests cover zero investment, no positive returns, unrecovered costs, discounted recovery and known IRR/NPV values. [Microsoft NPV timing guidance](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/excel/functions/npv-function).

Implemented now: combined province/rating/status savings filters with filtered KPI totals, clear-filter control and empty results. Properties already combined search/province/rating and sort. Initiative cards already had editable status dropdowns.

Already implemented: submission validation for completed consecutive reporting months, areas, reference/methodology, technical observations, professional/audit fields and evidence; enforcement occurs in the private server and browser adapter. This is a project completeness gate, not an independent legal determination of compliance with licensed SANS clauses. Private audit records already contain user ID/time and changed field paths for assessment edits. Browser records now also identify changed paths and the local actor; browser audit can be altered by the device user and is not an immutable inspection-body ledger.

Corrections: multiplying avoided emissions by a carbon tax rate does not establish tradable credits, cash revenue or the building owner's tax benefit. Approved project eligibility, verification, registry treatment, ownership and applicable allowance conditions need separate evidence. [Treasury carbon-offset eligibility and double-benefit clarification](https://www.gov.za/news/media-statements/national-treasury-amendments-carbon-offsets-regulations-20-jul-2021), [SARS carbon tax policy](https://www.sars.gov.za/wp-content/uploads/Ops/Policies/SE-CBT-02-Carbon-Tax-External-Policy.pdf). No current tax rate is hardcoded into the scenarios.

Certificate workflow: [SANEDI September 2024 guidance](https://epc.sanedi.org.za/EPC%20Guideline%20_%20V3_September%202024.pdf) describes the move from SANAS inspection bodies to registered professionals after July 2024. The research statement that every application must go through a SANAS body is incomplete. Use current SANEDI registration guidance for issuer checks. Existing review PDFs remain assessment review packs; this update does not copy the official national certificate, claim direct NBEPR integration or fabricate an issued EPC.

Production-ready and ironclad-compliance claims in the supplied feedback are not established by the screenshot or this change. The Pages workspace stores records locally; authenticated AWS runtime, operational ownership and professional acceptance remain separate deployment and governance work.
